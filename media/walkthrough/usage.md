# Your Claude usage, always in view

At the bottom right, next to your account:

**`5h 2% · wk 60%`**

- **5h**: how much of the current 5-hour Claude limit is used
- **wk**: how much of the weekly limit is used
- Same numbers as Claude Code's `/usage`
- Yellow box from 80% used, red from 95%

**Hover** it to see every account: bars, reset times, tokens and email, plus the Other AI models you used.

**Click** it to open the **Hoppy Usage** view in the side bar.
