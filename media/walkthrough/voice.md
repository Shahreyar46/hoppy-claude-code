# Talk instead of typing

1. Click **Voice** at the bottom right (or press `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+V`)
2. Speak
3. Click again

The text appears in the Claude chat box, **not sent**: check it, then press Enter.

| You speak | You need |
|---|---|
| English, Hindi, Spanish and 17 more | Nothing: uses Claude Code's own dictation |
| Bangla or any other language | Your Gemini or Groq key from Hoppy settings |

**Translate:** hover **Voice** → **Write in** → for example **English**. Speak Bangla, get English.
